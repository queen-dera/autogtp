This document outlines the **4 core content pillars** for a **$7K SaaS Content Strategy** designed to generate **$50K+ in pipeline** within 90 days. Each pillar is strategically aligned with **business goals, audience needs, and revenue objectives** to create a cohesive, high-impact content ecosystem.

## Pillar 1: Thought Leadership

### Overview
**Purpose**: Establish your brand as an **industry authority**, build trust, and attract high-quality leads through **insightful, data-driven content**.

**Value Proposition**: "We don't just sell software; we shape the future of [your industry]."

**Budget Allocation**: 25% of content budget ($1,750 for $7K strategy)

### Goals & KPIs

| Goal | KPI | Target (90 days) | Measurement |
|------|-----|-------------------|-------------|
| Brand Authority | Brand search volume | +50% | Google Search Console |
| Audience Growth | LinkedIn followers | +1,000 | LinkedIn Analytics |
| Engagement | Average time on page | +30% | Google Analytics |
| Lead Quality | SQL conversion rate | +25% | CRM data |
| Media Mentions | Backlinks from industry sites | 20+ | Ahrefs/SEMrush |

### Content Types & Formats

#### Primary Formats (70% of pillar content)
1. **Industry Reports** (20%)
   - Comprehensive, data-driven analysis
   - Length: 3,000-5,000 words
   - Example: "The State of SaaS Growth in 2024"
   - **ROI**: High lead capture, backlink magnet

2. **Trend Predictions** (15%)
   - Forward-looking insights
   - Length: 1,500-2,500 words
   - Example: "5 SaaS Trends That Will Dominate 2025"
   - **ROI**: High social shares, media pickups

3. **Expert Interviews** (15%)
   - Conversations with industry leaders
   - Length: 2,000-3,000 words (or 30-60 min video)
   - Example: "How [Industry Leader] Scaled from $1M to $10M ARR"
   - **ROI**: High engagement, credibility boost

#### Secondary Formats (30% of pillar content)
1. **Opinion Pieces** (10%)
   - Controversial or contrarian takes
   - Length: 1,000-2,000 words
   - Example: "Why Most SaaS Companies Are Doing Growth Wrong"
   - **ROI**: High engagement, debate spark

2. **Data Visualizations** (10%)
   - Infographics, charts, interactive tools
   - Example: "The SaaS Growth Metrics Dashboard"
   - **ROI**: High shareability, backlinks

3. **Newsletters** (10%)
   - Curated industry insights
   - Example: "Weekly SaaS Growth Insights"
   - **ROI**: High open rates, lead nurturing

### Topic Clusters for Thought Leadership

#### Cluster 1: Industry Trends

#### Cluster 3: Metrics & Benchmarks

- "How Your Growth Metrics Compare to Industry Standards"
- "The Ultimate SaaS Benchmarks Report"
- "How to Calculate CAC, LTV, and Other Key Metrics"
- "The Metrics That Predict SaaS Success"

#### Cluster 4: Leadership & Culture
- "How to Build a Growth Culture"
- "The Role of Leadership in SaaS Growth"
- "How to Hire for Growth Mindset"
- "The Best Growth Teams in SaaS (And What You Can Learn)"
