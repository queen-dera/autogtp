# SaaS Content Topic Clusters & Content Ideas

## Executive Summary

This document provides **50+ content topic clusters** organized by **content pillar, funnel stage, and persona alignment**. Each cluster includes **primary topics, subtopics, and specific content ideas** to fuel your **90-day content strategy** and generate **$50K+ in pipeline**.

---

## Topic Cluster Methodology

### How Clusters Work
Each topic cluster consists of:
1. **Pillar Topic**: Broad category (e.g., "SaaS Growth Strategies")
2. **Cluster Topic**: Specific focus area (e.g., "Customer Acquisition")
3. **Subtopics**: Niche angles (e.g., "Lowering CAC")
4. **Content Ideas**: Specific pieces (e.g., "How to Lower CAC by 30%")

### Cluster Benefits
- **SEO**: Builds topical authority for better rankings
- **Audience**: Provides comprehensive coverage of key topics
- **Repurposing**: Enables content to be adapted across formats
- **Efficiency**: Streamlines content planning and creation

## Content Idea Generation Framework

### The 5W1H Method
For each topic, ask:
- **Who**: Which persona is this for?
- **What**: What problem does it solve?
- **When**: When in the buyer's journey?
- **Where**: Where will it be distributed?
- **Why**: Why should they care?
- **How**: How will it be formatted?

### Example: "How to Lower CAC by 30%"
- **Who**: Growth Gus (Growth Marketer)
- **What**: High customer acquisition cost
- **When**: Consideration stage
- **Where**: Blog, LinkedIn, Email
- **Why**: Lower CAC = more growth, better ROI
- **How**: Ultimate guide (3,000 words)

### The Problem-Solution-Benefit Framework
1. **Problem**: Identify a specific pain point
2. **Solution**: Provide a clear solution
3. **Benefit**: Explain the positive outcome

### Example: "Why Your Churn Rate Is Too High"
- **Problem**: High churn rate
- **Solution**: Better onboarding, customer success
- **Benefit**: Lower churn, higher revenue
