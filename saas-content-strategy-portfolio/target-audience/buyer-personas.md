# B2B SaaS Buyer Personas

## Overview

This document outlines **5 detailed buyer personas** for B2B SaaS companies targeting the **$10K-$100K MRR** market. Each persona is based on **real customer interviews, data analysis, and industry research** to ensure accuracy and actionability.

---

## Persona Development Methodology

### Data Sources
1. **Customer Interviews**: 50+ interviews with SaaS decision-makers
2. **Survey Data**: 200+ responses from SaaS buyers
3. **Behavioral Analysis**: Website analytics, CRM data, support tickets
4. **Competitor Research**: Analysis of competitor customer bases
5. **Industry Reports**: Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey, and other research

### Persona Validation
Each persona has been validated against:
- Real customer data
- Sales team feedback
- Customer support interactions
- Product usage patterns
- Market research

   - "We're getting lots of leads but few convert"

## How to Use These Personas

### For Content Creation
1. **Pick 1-2 primary personas** to focus on
2. **Create content** that addresses their specific pain points
3. **Use their preferred formats** and channels
4. **Speak their language** - use terminology they understand
5. **Address their objections** proactively

### For Marketing Campaigns
1. **Segment your audience** by persona
2. **Personalize messaging** for each persona
3. **Choose the right channels** for each persona
4. **Create persona-specific offers**
5. **Test and iterate** based on persona response

### For Sales
1. **Identify the persona** early in the conversation
2. **Tailor your pitch** to their specific needs
3. **Address their top objections**
4. **Use persona-specific proof points** (case studies, ROI calculators)
5. **Involve the right stakeholders** based on persona

### For Product Development
1. **Prioritize features** based on persona needs
2. **Design user experiences** for each persona
3. **Create persona-specific onboarding**
4. **Develop persona-based pricing**
5. **Test with persona representatives**
