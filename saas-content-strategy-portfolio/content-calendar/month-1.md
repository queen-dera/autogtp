# 90-Day SaaS Content Calendar - Month 1

## Overview

This document outlines **Weeks 1-4** of your **90-day SaaS content strategy**, designed to generate **$50K+ in pipeline**. Month 1 focuses on **foundation building, quick wins, and establishing momentum** across all content pillars.

---

## Month 1 Goals

### Primary Objectives
1. **Launch Content Engine**: Establish consistent content production
2. **Quick Wins**: Publish high-impact, high-intent content
3. **Audience Building**: Grow LinkedIn following by 500+
4. **Lead Generation**: Generate first 50 MQLs
5. **SEO Foundation**: Index first 20 content pieces

## Content Promotion Plan

### Organic Promotion

#### Social Media
- **LinkedIn**: Share each blog post 2-3x (initial post, follow-up, engagement)
- **Twitter**: Share each post 3-5x with different angles
- **Facebook**: Share top-performing content 1-2x
- **Reddit**: Share relevant content in niche subreddits (1-2x/week)

#### Email
- **Newsletter**: Include 2-3 new pieces in weekly newsletter
- **Dedicated Emails**: Send for high-value content (case studies, guides)
- **Sales Enablement**: Share relevant content with sales team for outreach

#### Community
- **Slack/Discord**: Share in relevant communities (1-2x/week)
- **Forums**: Engage in industry forums with helpful content
- **Comments**: Respond to comments on all published content

### Paid Promotion

#### Week 3-4 Tests
1. **LinkedIn Ads**
   - Target: High-intent keywords from top-performing content
   - Budget: $500
   - Goal: Lead generation

2. **Google Ads**
   - Target: Bottom-funnel keywords (competitor comparisons, ROI)
   - Budget: $500
   - Goal: Conversions

3. **Retargeting**
   - Target: Blog visitors who didn't convert
   - Budget: $300
   - Goal: Return visits, conversions

### Content Repurposing

#### Week 2-4 Repurposing
1. **Blog to LinkedIn**: Turn key points from blog posts into LinkedIn posts
2. **Blog to Twitter**: Create tweet threads from blog content
3. **Blog to Email**: Adapt blog content for email newsletters
4. **Guide to Checklist**: Turn ultimate guides into downloadable checklists
5. **Case Study to Social**: Create social media snippets from case studies
