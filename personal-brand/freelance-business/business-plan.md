## Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Models

#### 1. Value-Based Pricing (Recommended)
**Approach**: Price based on the **value you deliver**, not the time you spend
**Formula**: 
```
Client's Expected ROI × Your Value Share = Your Price
```
**Example**: If your content strategy generates $50K in pipeline and you take 10% credit:
```
$50,000 × 10% = $5,000/month
```

**Pros**:
- Higher profits for high-impact work
- Aligns your incentives with client's success
- Justifies premium pricing

**Cons**:
- Harder to sell (need to prove ROI)
- Requires strong case studies

#### 2. Retainer Pricing
**Approach**: Fixed monthly fee for ongoing services
**Example**: $7,000/month for content strategy + 8 blog posts + distribution

**Pros**:
- Predictable revenue
- Builds long-term relationships
- Encourages ongoing optimization

**Cons**:
- Requires consistent delivery
- Scope creep can be an issue

#### 3. Project-Based Pricing
**Approach**: Fixed price for defined scope of work
**Example**: $5,000 for content strategy development (one-time)

**Pros**:
- Clear expectations
- Easy to sell
- Good for one-time projects

**Cons**:
- Less predictable revenue
- No recurring income

#### 4. Hourly Pricing (Not Recommended)
**Approach**: Charge by the hour
**Example**: $200/hour

**Pros**:
- Simple to understand
- Good for small, undefined projects

**Cons**:
- Limits earning potential
- Clients focus on hours, not results
- Hard to scale

3. **Their Goals** (10 min): What they want to achieve with content
4. **Their Challenges** (10 min): Current pain points and obstacles
5. **Your Solution** (5 min): How you can help
6. **Next Steps** (5 min): Proposal, timeline, pricing

**Discovery Call Questions:**
- "What are your top 3 business goals for the next 12 months?"
- "What's your current content marketing strategy?"
- "What's working well with your content right now?"
- "What's not working or missing from your content strategy?"
- "Who is your ideal customer?"
- "What are your top 3 pain points with content marketing?"
- "What's your budget for content marketing?"
- "What's your timeline for seeing results?"
- "Who would be the main point of contact?"
- "What does success look like for you?"

**Discovery Call Tips:**
✅ **Listen more than you talk** (80/20 rule)
✅ **Ask open-ended questions** (get them talking)
✅ **Take detailed notes** (use for proposal)
✅ **Qualify the lead** (budget, authority, need, timeline)
✅ **Set clear next steps** (proposal, follow-up, etc.)

❌ **Avoid**:
- Pitching your services too early
- Talking too much about yourself
- Making promises you can't keep
- Not qualifying the lead properly

### Step 2: Proposal (2-5 pages)

**Proposal Structure:**
1. **Cover Page**: Your branding, client name, date
2. **Executive Summary**: Brief overview of the project
3. **Understanding of Needs**: Summary of their goals and challenges
4. **Proposed Solution**: Your approach and deliverables
5. **Timeline**: Project phases and milestones
6. **Pricing**: Investment and payment terms
7. **Next Steps**: How to move forward
8. **About You**: Your background and credentials
9. **Case Studies**: 2-3 relevant examples
10. **Testimonials**: Client quotes

**Proposal Tips:**
✅ **Customize for each client** (no generic proposals)
✅ **Focus on their goals** (not your services)
✅ **Include specific deliverables** (what they'll get)
✅ **Show ROI** (how this will benefit them)
✅ **Keep it concise** (2-5 pages max)
✅ **Use visuals** (charts, timelines, etc.)

❌ **Avoid**:
- Generic, copy-paste proposals
- Focusing on features over benefits
- Being too vague about deliverables
- Overpromising results
- Making it too long or complex

### Step 3: Contract & Payment

**Contract Essentials:**
1. **Scope of Work**: Detailed list of deliverables
2. **Timeline**: Start date, milestones, end date
3. **Pricing**: Total investment and payment schedule
4. **Payment Terms**: Deposit, milestones, final payment
5. **Revisions**: Number of included revisions
6. **Termination**: Conditions for ending the contract
7. **Confidentiality**: NDA and data protection
8. **Ownership**: Who owns the work product
9. **Liability**: Limitation of liability
10. **Governing Law**: Jurisdiction for disputes

**Payment Terms:**
- **Deposit**: 30-50% upfront (non-refundable)
- **Milestones**: 30-50% upon delivery of key milestones
- **Final Payment**: 20-30% upon completion
- **Net Terms**: Net 15 or Net 30 for established clients

**Contract Tips:**
✅ **Use a template** (but customize for each client)
✅ **Be specific** about deliverables and timelines
✅ **Include payment terms** (protect yourself)
✅ **Set clear expectations** (avoid scope creep)
✅ **Get it signed** (before starting work)

❌ **Avoid**:
- Starting work without a signed contract
- Vague or ambiguous terms
- No payment protection
- Unlimited revisions
- No termination clause

## Risk Management

### Contracts & Legal
- **Always use a contract** (even for small projects)
- **Require a deposit** (30-50% upfront for new clients)
- **Limit liability** (include limitation of liability clause)
- **Protect IP** (clarify who owns the work product)
- **NDA** (for confidential information)
- **Consult a lawyer** (for contract review and legal advice)

### Client Selection
- **Qualify leads** (budget, authority, need, timeline)
- **Avoid bad clients** (red flags: unrealistic expectations, poor communication, history of not paying)
- **Trust your gut** (if something feels off, walk away)
- **Fire bad clients** (it's better to lose one bad client than keep them)

### Financial Protection
- **Deposit required** (non-refundable for custom work)
- **Milestone payments** (don't deliver all work before final payment)
- **Late fees** (charge 1.5% per month on overdue invoices)
- **Stop work** (on overdue accounts after 14 days)
- **Collections** (use a collections agency for severely overdue accounts)

### Reputation Management
- **Deliver quality** (always exceed expectations)
- **Be responsive** (quick turnaround on communication)
- **Handle complaints** (address issues promptly and professionally)
- **Ask for testimonials** (collect social proof from happy clients)
- **Monitor online reputation** (Google, LinkedIn, review sites)
