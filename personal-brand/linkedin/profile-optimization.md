- **Appears on your profile** for all visitors to see

## Step 9: Custom LinkedIn URL

### Why It Matters
- **Easier to share** (cleaner than the default URL)
- **More professional** (looks better on resumes and business cards)
- **Better for SEO** (can include your name)

### How to Customize Your URL
1. Go to your LinkedIn profile
2. Click "Edit public profile & URL" on the right sidebar
3. Click "Edit URL" under "Edit your custom URL"
4. Type in your custom URL (e.g., `linkedin.com/in/yourname`)
5. Click "Save"

### URL Tips
✅ **Use your name** (e.g., `linkedin.com/in/johndoe`)
✅ **Keep it simple** (no numbers or special characters)
✅ **Make it consistent** with your other professional profiles
✅ **Include it** on your resume, email signature, and business cards

❌ **Avoid**:
- Using the default URL (it's long and messy)
- Including numbers or special characters
- Making it too long or complicated

## Step 10: Open to Work (If Applicable)

### Why It Matters
- **Signals to recruiters** that you're actively looking
- **Increases visibility** in recruiter searches
- **Shows your preferences** (job types, locations, work arrangements)

### How to Set Up Open to Work
1. Click the "Open to" button below your headline
2. Select "Finding a new job"
3. Choose the job titles you're interested in (e.g., Content Strategist, Content Marketing Manager)
4. Select job locations (Remote, specific cities, etc.)
5. Choose work arrangements (Full-time, Contract, Freelance, etc.)
6. Select whether to share with all LinkedIn members or only recruiters
7. Add a note to recruiters (optional)

### Open to Work Tips
✅ **Be specific** about the roles you want (Content Strategist, not just "Marketer")
✅ **Select remote** if you're open to remote work
✅ **Choose "All LinkedIn members"** to maximize visibility (unless you prefer privacy)
✅ **Add a note** to recruiters with your target roles and key skills
✅ **Update regularly** as your preferences change

❌ **Avoid**:
- Being too vague ("Marketing" vs. "B2B SaaS Content Strategist")
- Selecting locations you're not actually open to
- Forgetting to update it when you land a role

❌ "I'm good at marketing."

❌ Missing sections (Experience, Skills, About, etc.)
✅ All sections completed with compelling content

❌ Profile exists but no posts, comments, or engagement
✅ Regularly publishes content and engages with others

### 8. Unprofessional Photo
❌ Selfie, group photo, or casual picture
✅ Professional headshot with neutral background
