# 30-60-90 Day Plan: B2B SaaS Content Marketing Specialist

## Executive Summary

This **30-60-90 Day Plan** is tailored for **Content Marketing Specialist roles** in **B2B SaaS companies**. It focuses on **execution, optimization, and lead generation** to demonstrate your **hands-on capabilities** and **immediate impact** on the company's content marketing efforts.

**Key Outcomes:**
- 30 Days: Audit, quick wins, and content calendar setup
- 60 Days: Consistent execution and performance optimization
- 90 Days: Lead generation, conversion improvement, and ROI tracking

---

## Phase 1: First 30 Days (Audit, Setup, Quick Wins)

### Goals
1. Understand current content marketing efforts
2. Identify quick wins for immediate impact
3. Set up systems and processes for success
4. Create a 30-day content calendar

### Week 1: Discovery & Onboarding

**Key Tasks:**
- **Meet with Marketing Team** (Days 1-2):
  - Current content calendar and publishing cadence
  - Top-performing content pieces and why
  - Biggest content marketing challenges
  - Tools and processes currently used

- **Meet with Sales Team** (Day 3):
  - Most used content in sales process
  - Missing content that would help close deals
  - Common objections and how content could address them

- **Content Audit** (Days 4-5):
  - Review last 6 months of content
  - Identify top performers by traffic, engagement, conversions
  - Note underperforming content and potential improvements

- **Tool Access** (Days 1-5):
  - CMS, email platform, social media tools
  - Analytics (Google Analytics, Search Console)
  - SEO tools (if available)

### Week 2: Quick Wins & Content Calendar

**Quick Wins to Implement:**
1. **SEO Optimization**: Update meta descriptions, title tags on top posts
2. **Content Repurposing**: Turn top blog posts into LinkedIn/Twitter content
3. **CTA Improvements**: Add/improve CTAs on high-traffic pages
4. **Email Capture**: Add lead magnets to popular content
5. **Social Sharing**: Improve shareability of content

**Content Calendar Setup:**
- Plan next 30 days of content
- Mix of blog posts, social content, emails
- Align with company goals and events
- Assign owners and deadlines

### Week 3: Process Documentation & Training

**Document Processes:**
- Content creation workflow
- Content distribution checklist
- Content measurement and reporting

**Train Team (if applicable):**
- Best practices for content creation
- SEO basics for content
- Social media promotion tips

### Week 4: First Content Execution

**Create and Publish:**
- 2-3 blog posts (1,500-2,000 words)
- 4-6 LinkedIn posts
- 1-2 email sequences
- 1 lead magnet (checklist, template, guide)

**Promotion Plan:**
- Share on all social channels
- Feature in email newsletter
- Engage with comments and shares
- Track performance metrics

---

## Phase 2: Days 31-60 (Consistent Execution & Optimization)

### Goals
1. Maintain consistent content publishing
2. Optimize based on performance data
3. Improve engagement and conversions
4. Expand content distribution

### Week 5: Content Production Ramp-Up

**Content Creation:**
- 4 blog posts (2,000-2,500 words)
- 8 LinkedIn posts
- 2 email sequences
- 1 downloadable resource

**Quality Assurance:**
- SEO optimization for all content
- Proofreading and editing
- Brand voice consistency
- CTA placement and effectiveness

### Week 6: Distribution & Promotion Expansion

**Social Media Strategy:**
- LinkedIn: 3-4 posts/week with engagement
- Twitter: 5-7 tweets/week
- Other platforms as relevant

**Email Marketing:**
- Weekly newsletter featuring new content
- Dedicated emails for high-value content
- Segmented emails for different audiences

**Paid Promotion (if budget allows):**
- Boost top-performing organic posts
- LinkedIn sponsored content
- Google Ads for high-intent keywords

### Week 7: Performance Analysis & Optimization

**Track Metrics:**
- Traffic by source and content piece
- Engagement (time on page, social shares)
- Conversions (form submissions, downloads)
- Lead quality (for content-driven leads)

**Optimization Actions:**
- Update underperforming content
- Improve headlines and meta descriptions
- A/B test CTAs and landing pages
- Adjust promotion strategy based on data

### Week 8: Content Repurposing & Scaling

**Repurpose Top Content:**
- Blog posts → LinkedIn carousels, Twitter threads
- Blog posts → Email newsletters
- Blog posts → Infographics or visual content
- Webinars → Blog posts, social clips

**Scale What Works:**
- Double down on high-performing topics
- Increase frequency of popular content types
- Expand distribution of top performers

---

## Phase 3: Days 61-90 (Lead Generation & ROI)

### Goals
1. Generate measurable pipeline from content
2. Improve conversion rates
3. Track and report ROI
4. Plan for next quarter

### Week 9: Lead Generation Focus

**Lead Magnet Creation:**
- Develop 2-3 high-value lead magnets:
  - Industry report or whitepaper
  - Template or checklist
  - Case study or ROI calculator

**Landing Page Optimization:**
- Improve existing landing pages
- Create dedicated pages for lead magnets
- A/B test different variations

**Email Nurture Sequences:**
- Create sequences for different lead types
- Align with buyer's journey stages
- Track conversion to opportunities

### Week 10: Sales Enablement

**Content for Sales Team:**
- Battle cards for competitive comparisons
- Case studies highlighting customer success
- ROI calculators or business case templates

**Sales Collaboration:**
- Regular meetings with sales team
- Feedback on content effectiveness
- Content requests for specific deals

### Week 11: ROI Tracking & Reporting

**Pipeline Attribution:**
- Track leads from content to pipeline
- Use UTM parameters and CRM tracking
- Attribute revenue to content efforts

**ROI Calculation:**
- Content creation costs
- Distribution and promotion costs
- Pipeline influenced by content
- Revenue attributed to content
- ROI = (Revenue - Investment) / Investment

**Reporting:**
- Weekly performance updates
- Monthly comprehensive reports
- Quarterly ROI analysis

### Week 12: Planning & Handoff

**90-Day Review:**
- What worked well
- What didn't perform
- Lessons learned
- Recommendations for next quarter

**Next Quarter Plan:**
- Content calendar for next 90 days
- Budget and resource needs
- Key initiatives and goals

**Knowledge Transfer:**
- Document all processes
- Train team members
- Hand off ongoing responsibilities
