# 30-60-90 Day Plan: Freelance B2B SaaS Content Client Onboarding

## Executive Summary

This **30-60-90 Day Plan** is designed for **onboarding new freelance clients** as a **B2B SaaS Content Strategist and Conversion Copywriter**. It ensures **quick wins, relationship building, and long-term strategy** to demonstrate value and secure long-term retainers.

**Key Outcomes:**
- 30 Days: Quick wins, trust building, foundation
- 60 Days: Strategy execution, initial results
- 90 Days: ROI demonstration, long-term planning

---

## Phase 1: First 30 Days (Quick Wins & Foundation)

### Goals
1. Build trust and rapport with client
2. Deliver immediate value with quick wins
3. Understand client's business, goals, and challenges
4. Set up systems and processes for success

### Week 1: Discovery & Kickoff

**Kickoff Meeting Agenda:**
1. **Introductions** (10 min)
   - Your background and expertise
   - Client's business and team

2. **Client's Business** (20 min)
   - Company overview, product, market
   - Current content marketing efforts
   - Business goals and challenges

3. **Your Approach** (20 min)
   - Your methodology and framework
   - What you'll deliver in first 30/60/90 days
   - How you'll measure success

4. **Q&A** (10 min)
   - Answer client questions
   - Address concerns

**Discovery Questions:**
```
Business & Goals:
- What are your top 3 business goals for the next 12 months?
- What's your current revenue, and what's your growth target?
- Who is your ideal customer?
- What are your biggest marketing challenges?

Content Marketing:
- What's your current content strategy?
- What's working well with your content right now?
- What's not working or missing?
- What tools and platforms do you use?
- What's your current content calendar and publishing cadence?

Team & Resources:
- Who will I be working with on your team?
- What's your team's bandwidth for content?
- What resources do you have available (budget, tools, etc.)?
- Who are the key stakeholders I should align with?

Measurement & Success:
- How do you currently measure content success?
- What does success look like for you?
- What are your target metrics (traffic, leads, pipeline, revenue)?
- What's your current content marketing ROI?
```

**Deliverables:**
- Kickoff meeting notes and action items
- Access to all necessary tools and platforms
- Initial content audit (quick assessment)

### Week 2: Content Audit & Quick Wins

**Quick Content Audit:**
- Review last 3 months of content
- Identify top performers by traffic, engagement, conversions
- Note underperforming content and opportunities
- Analyze SEO performance (if applicable)

**Quick Wins to Implement:**
1. **SEO Optimization**: Update meta descriptions, title tags on top posts
2. **Content Repurposing**: Turn top blog posts into social content
3. **CTA Improvements**: Add/improve CTAs on high-traffic pages
4. **Email Capture**: Add lead magnets to popular content
5. **Social Sharing**: Improve shareability of content

**Deliverables:**
- Quick wins implementation plan
- 2-3 quick wins executed
- Initial performance improvements tracked

### Week 3: Strategy Alignment & Planning

**Stakeholder Interviews (if applicable):**
- Sales team: Content needs, objections, sales cycle
- Product team: Differentiators, use cases, roadmap
- Customer success: Pain points, retention, churn

**Strategy Alignment:**
- Review client's business goals
- Align content strategy with business objectives
- Identify key themes and messages
- Define target audience and personas

**Deliverables:**
- Stakeholder interview notes
- Initial content strategy outline
- 30-day content plan

### Week 4: First Content Delivery

**Content Creation:**
- 1-2 blog posts (1,500-2,000 words)
- 2-3 LinkedIn posts
- 1 email sequence
- 1 lead magnet (checklist, template, guide)

**Promotion Plan:**
- Share on client's social channels
- Feature in client's email newsletter
- Engage with comments and shares

**Deliverables:**
- First set of content delivered
- Content published and promoted
- Initial performance metrics tracked

## Phase 2: Days 31-60 (Strategy Execution & Results)

### Goals
1. Execute on the content strategy
2. Build momentum with consistent delivery
3. Optimize based on performance data
4. Demonstrate initial results

### Week 5: Strategy Presentation & Approval

**Strategy Presentation Outline:**
1. **Recap of Discovery** (5 min)
   - Key insights from audit and interviews
   - Quick wins implemented and results

2. **Proposed Strategy** (15 min)
   - Content pillars and themes
   - Target audience and personas
   - Keyword and SEO strategy
   - Distribution and promotion plan

3. **90-Day Plan** (10 min)
   - Content calendar overview
   - Key milestones and deadlines
   - Resource requirements

4. **Measurement & Reporting** (5 min)
   - KPIs and success metrics
   - Reporting cadence and format

5. **Q&A** (5 min)
   - Answer questions
   - Address concerns
   - Align on next steps

**Deliverables:**
- Strategy presentation deck
- Strategy document (detailed)
- Approval from client

### Week 6: Content Production & Publishing

**Content Creation:**
- 3-4 blog posts (2,000-2,500 words)
- 6-8 LinkedIn posts
- 2 email sequences
- 1 downloadable resource

**Quality Assurance:**
- SEO optimization
- Proofreading and editing
- Brand voice alignment
- CTA effectiveness

**Deliverables:**
- Consistent content delivery
- All content published on time
- Performance tracking set up

### Week 7: Distribution & Promotion Expansion

**Social Media Strategy:**
- LinkedIn: 3-4 posts/week with engagement
- Twitter: 5-7 tweets/week
- Other platforms as relevant

**Email Marketing:**
- Weekly newsletter featuring new content
- Dedicated emails for high-value content

**Paid Promotion (if budget allows):**
- Boost top-performing organic posts
- LinkedIn sponsored content

**Deliverables:**
- Expanded distribution reach
- Increased engagement metrics
- Growing audience

### Week 8: Performance Analysis & Optimization

**Performance Tracking:**
- Traffic by source and content piece
- Engagement (time on page, social shares)
- Conversions (form submissions, downloads)
- Lead quality (for content-driven leads)

**Optimization Actions:**
- Update underperforming content
- Improve headlines and meta descriptions
- A/B test CTAs
- Adjust promotion strategy

**Deliverables:**
- Monthly performance report
- Optimization recommendations
- Client review and approval

## Phase 3: Days 61-90 (ROI Demonstration & Long-Term Planning)

### Goals
1. Demonstrate measurable ROI
2. Secure long-term retainer
3. Plan for ongoing success
4. Set up for scale

### Week 9: ROI Focus & Lead Generation

**Lead Magnet Creation:**
- Develop 1-2 high-value lead magnets:
  - Industry report or whitepaper
  - Template or checklist
  - Case study

**Landing Page Optimization:**
- Improve existing landing pages
- Create dedicated pages for lead magnets
- A/B test different variations

**Email Nurture Sequences:**
- Create sequences for different lead types
- Align with buyer's journey
- Track conversion to opportunities

**Deliverables:**
- New lead generation assets
- Improved conversion rates
- Growing pipeline

### Week 10: Sales Enablement & Collaboration

**Content for Sales Team:**
- Battle cards for competitive comparisons
- Case studies highlighting customer success
- ROI calculators or business case templates

**Sales Collaboration:**
- Regular check-ins with sales team
- Feedback on content effectiveness
- Content requests for specific deals

**Deliverables:**
- Sales enablement content
- Improved sales-content alignment
- Better content for sales process

### Week 11: Comprehensive ROI Reporting

**Pipeline Attribution:**
- Track leads from content to pipeline
- Use UTM parameters and CRM tracking
- Attribute revenue to content efforts

**ROI Calculation:**
- Content creation costs
- Distribution and promotion costs
- Pipeline influenced by content
- Revenue attributed to content
- ROI = (Revenue - Investment) / Investment

**Deliverables:**
- Comprehensive ROI report
- Pipeline and revenue attribution
- Clear business impact demonstration

### Week 12: Long-Term Planning & Retainer Discussion

**90-Day Review:**
- What worked well
- What didn't perform
- Lessons learned
- Recommendations for next quarter

**Long-Term Strategy:**
- 6-12 month content strategy
- Budget and resource needs
- Key initiatives and goals
- Retainer proposal (if applicable)

**Retainer Discussion:**
- Present results and ROI
- Discuss ongoing needs
- Propose retainer terms and pricing
- Secure long-term commitment

**Deliverables:**
- 90-day comprehensive report
- Long-term strategy document
- Retainer proposal (if applicable)
- Client satisfaction survey
