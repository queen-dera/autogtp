# 30-60-90 Day Plan: Fractional B2B SaaS Content Leader

## Executive Summary

This **30-60-90 Day Plan** is designed for **Fractional Content Leadership roles** (e.g., Fractional CMO, Fractional Head of Content) in **B2B SaaS companies**. It focuses on **strategic direction, team leadership, and cross-functional alignment** to demonstrate your **executive-level impact** in a part-time capacity.

**Key Outcomes:**
- 30 Days: Assessment, alignment, quick wins
- 60 Days: Strategy development, team building, execution
- 90 Days: Measurable impact, long-term roadmap, ROI

---

## Phase 1: First 30 Days (Assessment & Alignment)

### Goals
1. Assess current state of content marketing
2. Align with executive team on goals
3. Identify quick wins for immediate impact
4. Develop high-level strategy framework

### Week 1: Executive Alignment

**Stakeholder Meetings:**
- **CEO** (Day 1):
  - Company vision and goals
  - Biggest challenges and opportunities
  - Expectations for content marketing
  - Success metrics

- **CMO/Head of Marketing** (Day 2):
  - Current marketing strategy
  - Content marketing role in overall strategy
  - Team structure and resources
  - Budget and tools

- **VP of Sales** (Day 3):
  - Sales process and cycle
  - Content needs for sales enablement
  - Common objections and pain points
  - Collaboration opportunities

- **Head of Product** (Day 4):
  - Product roadmap and priorities
  - Differentiators and use cases
  - Content needs for product launches
  - Customer feedback and insights

**Company Deep Dive:**
- Review company strategy and goals
- Analyze current content marketing efforts
- Assess team capabilities and resources
- Review tools, platforms, and budgets

### Week 2: Content Audit & Quick Wins

**Comprehensive Content Audit:**
- Content inventory (all assets, formats, channels)
- Performance analysis (traffic, engagement, conversions)
- SEO audit (rankings, traffic, technical issues)
- Competitive analysis (competitor strategies and gaps)

**Quick Wins Identification:**
- Low-hanging fruit for immediate impact
- High-impact, low-effort opportunities
- Alignment with business goals

**Quick Wins Examples:**
1. Repurpose top-performing content
2. Optimize high-traffic pages for conversions
3. Align content with sales priorities
4. Improve content distribution and promotion
5. Enhance lead capture and nurturing

### Week 3: Team Assessment & Strategy Framework

**Team Assessment:**
- Current team structure and roles
- Skills and capabilities assessment
- Bandwidth and resource analysis
- Training and development needs

**Strategy Framework Development:**
- High-level content strategy outline
- Content pillars and themes
- Target audience and personas
- Keyword and SEO strategy
- Distribution and promotion plan

**Deliverables:**
- Team assessment report
- Initial strategy framework
- Quick wins implementation plan

### Week 4: Strategy Presentation & Approval

**Executive Presentation:**
1. **Current State Assessment** (10 min)
   - Content audit findings
   - Team assessment
   - Quick wins identified

2. **Proposed Strategy** (15 min)
   - Content pillars and themes
   - Target audience and goals
   - Key initiatives and priorities
   - Expected outcomes and ROI

3. **90-Day Plan** (10 min)
   - Key milestones and deliverables
   - Resource requirements
   - Timeline and dependencies

4. **Q&A** (10 min)
   - Address concerns and questions
   - Align on priorities and next steps

**Deliverables:**
- Executive presentation deck
- Strategy framework document
- 90-day plan outline
- Approval from executive team

## Phase 2: Days 31-60 (Strategy Execution & Team Building)

### Goals
1. Execute on the content strategy
2. Build and align the content team
3. Implement systems and processes
4. Demonstrate early results

### Week 5: Strategy Rollout & Team Alignment

**Strategy Communication:**
- Present strategy to full marketing team
- Align on goals, priorities, and expectations
- Assign roles and responsibilities
- Set up regular check-ins and reporting

**Process Implementation:**
- Content creation workflow
- Content distribution process
- Content measurement and reporting
- Content optimization framework

**Deliverables:**
- Team alignment meeting
- Process documentation
- Content calendar (first 90 days)

### Week 6: Team Building & Development

**Team Structure:**
- Define roles and responsibilities
- Assign content creation and distribution tasks
- Set up collaboration and communication channels

**Training & Development:**
- Content strategy training for team
- SEO best practices workshop
- Conversion optimization training
- Tool and platform training

**Hiring (if needed):**
- Identify gaps in team capabilities
- Develop job descriptions for new roles
- Source and interview candidates
- Onboard new team members

**Deliverables:**
- Team structure and roles defined
- Training sessions completed
- New hires onboarded (if applicable)

### Week 7: Content Production & Execution

**Content Creation:**
- Oversee creation of 8-12 content pieces
- Ensure alignment with strategy and brand voice
- Maintain quality and consistency

**Content Distribution:**
- Multi-channel distribution plan
- Social media promotion
- Email marketing integration
- Paid promotion (if applicable)

**Deliverables:**
- Consistent content delivery
- All content published on time
- Performance tracking set up

### Week 8: Performance Analysis & Optimization

**Performance Tracking:**
- Traffic by source and content piece
- Engagement metrics (time on page, social shares)
- Conversion metrics (form submissions, downloads)
- Pipeline and revenue attribution

**Optimization Actions:**
- Content optimization based on performance
- A/B testing for high-impact pages
- Strategy refinements based on data

**Deliverables:**
- Monthly performance report
- Optimization recommendations
- Strategy adjustments

**Team & Resource Planning:**

**Deliverables:**

### Week 12: Executive Review & Next Steps

**Executive Presentation:**

   - ROI and business value

3. **Long-Term Strategy** (10 min)
   - 6-12 month roadmap
   - Key initiatives and priorities
   - Resource requirements

4. **Recommendations** (5 min)
   - What to double down on
   - What to improve or stop
   - What to test next

5. **Q&A** (5 min)
   - Address questions and concerns
   - Discuss next steps

**Deliverables:**
- 90-day comprehensive report
- Long-term strategy presentation
- Executive review and approval
- Next steps and action items

## Fractional Leadership Model

### Time Allocation (15-20 hours/week)

| Activity | Time/Week | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Strategy & Planning | 3-4 hours | High-level direction, roadmap |
| Team Leadership | 3-4 hours | Meetings, training, feedback |
| Content Review | 2-3 hours | Review and approve content |
| Cross-Functional Collaboration | 2-3 hours | Sales, product, customer success |
| Reporting & Analysis | 2-3 hours | Performance tracking, ROI |
| Executive Updates | 1-2 hours | Presentations, meetings |

### Pricing Models

**1. Part-Time Fractional (10-15 hours/week):**
- **Price:** $5,000-$8,000/month
- **Focus:** Strategy, team leadership, high-level direction
- **Deliverables:** Strategy, roadmap, team alignment, reporting

**2. Part-Time Fractional (15-20 hours/week):**
- **Price:** $8,000-$12,000/month
- **Focus:** Strategy, execution, team leadership
- **Deliverables:** Strategy, content creation, distribution, reporting

**3. Full-Time Fractional (20-25 hours/week):**
- **Price:** $12,000-$15,000/month
- **Focus:** Full content leadership, execution, optimization
- **Deliverables:** Strategy, content engine, team building, ROI

### Engagement Terms

**Standard Terms:**
- **Commitment:** 3-12 month initial engagement
- **Notice Period:** 30-60 days for termination
- **Payment:** Monthly retainer, net 15 or 30
- **Expenses:** Client covers tools, software, etc.

**Success-Based Terms (Optional):**
- **Base Fee:** $5,000-$8,000/month
- **Performance Bonus:** 5-10% of pipeline/revenue attributed to content
- **Equity:** 0.1-0.5% equity for long-term engagements
