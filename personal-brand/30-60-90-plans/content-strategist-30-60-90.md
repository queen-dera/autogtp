# 30-60-90 Day Plan: B2B SaaS Content Strategist

## Executive Summary

This **30-60-90 Day Plan** is designed for **Content Strategist roles** in **B2B SaaS companies**. It demonstrates your **strategic thinking** and **immediate impact** on content marketing efforts.

**Key Outcomes:**
- 30 Days: Audit, align, quick wins
- 60 Days: Strategy execution & initial results
- 90 Days: Optimization, scaling, ROI measurement

---

## Phase 1: First 30 Days (Audit, Align, Quick Wins)

### Goals
1. Understand business, product, market, goals
2. Audit existing content
3. Align with stakeholders
4. Deliver quick wins

### Week 1: Discovery & Onboarding

**Stakeholder Interviews** (Days 1-3):
- Executive Leadership: Business goals, revenue targets, ICP
- Marketing Team: Current strategy, challenges, tools
- Sales Team: Content needs, objections, sales cycle
- Product Team: Differentiators, use cases, roadmap
- Customer Success: Customer pain points, retention

**Company Research Checklist:**
- [ ] Website, product pages, pricing analysis
- [ ] Blog content audit (topics, quality, performance)
- [ ] LinkedIn profile and posts review
- [ ] Email marketing analysis
- [ ] Case studies and testimonials review
- [ ] Competitor content analysis
- [ ] SEO performance (rankings, traffic, backlinks)
- [ ] Social media presence

**Tools to Request:**
- Google Analytics, Search Console
- CMS, email platform, social tools
- SEO tools (Ahrefs/SEMrush)
- CRM, project management tools

### Week 2: Content Audit & Gap Analysis

**Content Inventory Template:**
```
| ID | Title | Type | Date | Traffic | Engagement | Conversions | Notes |
```

**Performance Analysis Metrics:**
- Traffic: Page views, sessions, bounce rate
- Engagement: Time on page, social shares, comments
- Conversion: Form submissions, downloads, demo requests
- SEO: Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks

**Gap Analysis Focus:**
- Topic gaps (missing content on key areas)
- Format gaps (missing videos, podcasts, etc.)
- Funnel gaps (missing content for buyer journey stages)
- SEO gaps (missing keyword opportunities)

### Week 3: Stakeholder Alignment & Quick Wins

**Quick Wins Criteria:**
- Can be implemented in <1 week
- Requires minimal resources
- Has clear, measurable impact
- Aligns with business goals

**Quick Wins Examples:**
1. SEO optimization of top blog posts
2. Content repurposing (blog → LinkedIn, email)
3. CTA optimization on high-traffic pages
4. Internal linking improvements
5. Email capture forms on content

### Week 4: Strategy Development

**Deliverables:**
- ICP & buyer personas
- 4 content pillars with themes
- 25-30 topic clusters
- 90-day content strategy
- Final stakeholder presentation

---

## Phase 2: Days 31-60 (Execution & Initial Results)

### Goals
1. Implement the strategy
2. Build momentum with consistent content
3. Optimize based on performance data
4. Expand reach and engagement

### Week 5: Strategy Rollout
- Present strategy to full team
- Train team on new processes
- Document content creation/distribution processes
- Set up all tools and platforms
- Create first content briefs

### Week 6: Content Creation
**Content Brief Template:**
```
Title, Type, Pillar, Cluster, Keywords
Target Audience, Funnel Stage
Word Count, Tone, Style, Format
Outline (Intro, Sections, Conclusion)
SEO Requirements, Promotion Plan
Success Metrics
```

### Week 7: Distribution & Promotion
- Social media promotion (LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.)
- Email promotion (newsletter, dedicated emails)
- Paid promotion (if applicable)
- Influencer outreach
- Content repurposing

### Week 8: Measurement & Optimization
- Track all content performance
- Create weekly performance reports
- Set up A/B tests
- Optimize underperforming content
- Update stakeholders on progress

---

## Phase 3: Days 61-90 (Optimization, Scaling, ROI)

### Goals
1. Optimize performance based on data
2. Scale what works
3. Measure and report ROI
4. Plan for long-term success

### Week 9: Deep Dive & Optimization
- Comprehensive performance analysis
- A/B test results analysis
- Content optimization
- Strategy refinement
- Stakeholder review

### Week 10: Scaling & Expansion
- Scale high-performing content types
- Expand distribution channels
- Partnership outreach
- Content repurposing
- Team expansion (if needed)

### Week 11: ROI Measurement
**Pipeline Tracking Template:**
```
| Content | Leads | Pipeline | Deals | Revenue |
```

**ROI Calculation:**
- Total Investment (creation, distribution, tools)
- Total Revenue Attributed
- ROI = (Revenue - Investment) / Investment

### Week 12: Long-Term Planning
- 90-day review (what worked, what didn't)
- 6-12 month strategy development
- Team handoff
- Final report and presentation

## Success Metrics

### Leading Indicators (30-60 Days)
- Content published: 10-15 (30d), 25-30 (60d), 40-50 (90d)
- Organic traffic: +50% (30d), +100% (60d), +150% (90d)
- Engagement: Time on page +20%, bounce rate -15%
- SEO: Top 10 for 50+ keywords
- Conversions: +200% form submissions

### Lagging Indicators (90 Days+)
- Pipeline influenced: $50K-$200K
- Revenue attributed: $25K-$100K
- Lead-to-customer rate: 15-20%
- ROI: 8-12x
