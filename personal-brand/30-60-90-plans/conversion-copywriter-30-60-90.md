# 30-60-90 Day Plan: B2B SaaS Conversion Copywriter

## Executive Summary

This **30-60-90 Day Plan** is designed for **Conversion Copywriter roles** in **B2B SaaS companies**. It focuses on **messaging, A/B testing, and conversion optimization** to demonstrate your **specialized skills** in turning visitors into leads and customers.

**Key Outcomes:**
- 30 Days: Audit, messaging alignment, quick wins
- 60 Days: A/B testing, conversion optimization
- 90 Days: Measurable impact on conversion rates and revenue

---

## Phase 1: First 30 Days (Audit, Research, Quick Wins)

### Goals
1. Understand product, audience, and current messaging
2. Audit existing copy and conversion points
3. Identify quick wins for immediate impact
4. Develop messaging framework

### Week 1: Product & Audience Research

**Product Deep Dive:**
- Product demo and walkthrough
- Feature list and benefits
- Competitive differentiators
- Common use cases
- Customer pain points it solves

**Audience Research:**
- Review existing buyer personas
- Interview sales and customer success teams
- Analyze customer feedback and reviews
- Study competitor messaging

**Current Messaging Audit:**
- Website copy (homepage, product pages, pricing)
- Landing pages and CTAs
- Email sequences
- Ad copy
- Social media posts

### Week 2: Conversion Audit & Quick Wins

**Conversion Points to Audit:**
1. **Website:**
   - Homepage headline and value proposition
   - Product page copy
   - Pricing page messaging
   - CTAs (buttons, links, forms)
   - Landing pages

2. **Emails:**
   - Subject lines
   - Body copy
   - CTAs
   - Sequence flow

3. **Ads:**
   - Headlines
   - Body copy
   - CTAs
   - Targeting alignment

**Quick Wins to Implement:**
1. A/B test homepage headline
2. Improve CTA button copy (action-oriented language)
3. Shorten and clarify product descriptions
4. Add social proof (testimonials, logos, case studies)
5. Improve form copy and reduce friction

### Week 3: Messaging Framework Development

**Value Proposition Workshop:**
- Unique value proposition (UVP) for each product
- Key benefits and differentiators
- Customer pain points and how we solve them
- Competitive positioning

**Messaging Hierarchy:**
1. **Headline:** Primary value proposition (5-10 words)
2. **Subheadline:** Supporting benefit statement (10-20 words)
3. **Body Copy:** Key features and benefits (50-100 words)
4. **CTA:** Clear next step (2-5 words)

**Brand Voice Guidelines:**
- Tone (professional, conversational, technical, etc.)
- Style (concise, benefit-focused, data-driven, etc.)
- Formatting preferences (bullet points, bold text, etc.)

### Week 4: First Conversion Optimization Projects

**Project 1: Homepage Redesign**
- New headline and subheadline
- Revised value proposition
- Improved CTA placement and copy
- Social proof integration

**Project 2: Email Sequence Optimization**
- Subject line improvements
- Body copy refinements
- CTA optimization
- Sequence flow adjustments

**Project 3: Landing Page Creation**
- For new campaign or lead magnet
- Clear value proposition
- Benefit-focused copy
- Strong CTA

## Phase 2: Days 31-60 (A/B Testing & Optimization)

### Goals
1. Implement systematic A/B testing
2. Optimize high-impact conversion points
3. Improve messaging consistency
4. Measure and iterate based on data

### Week 5: A/B Testing Setup

**Testing Framework:**
- Hypothesis: What we believe will improve conversions
- Variation: What we're testing (A vs. B)
- Metric: What we're measuring (CTR, conversion rate, etc.)
- Duration: How long to run the test (1-2 weeks typically)
- Sample Size: Minimum visitors needed for statistical significance

**A/B Test Ideas:**
1. **Headlines:**
   - Benefit-focused vs. feature-focused
   - Problem vs. solution framing
   - Different value propositions

2. **CTAs:**
   - Button color and size
   - Button copy ("Get Started" vs. "Try for Free")
   - Placement on page

3. **Forms:**
   - Number of fields
   - Field labels
   - Submit button copy

4. **Social Proof:**
   - Testimonials vs. logos
   - Placement on page
   - Format (text vs. video)

5. **Pricing Page:**
   - Pricing presentation (monthly vs. annual)
   - Feature lists vs. benefit statements
   - CTA placement and copy

### Week 6: High-Impact Optimization

**Priority Pages for Optimization:**
1. Homepage (highest traffic)
2. Product pages (high intent)
3. Pricing page (conversion critical)
4. Top 3 landing pages
5. Email templates

**Optimization Checklist:**
- [ ] Clear value proposition in headline
- [ ] Benefit-focused body copy
- [ ] Strong, action-oriented CTA
- [ ] Social proof (testimonials, logos, etc.)
- [ ] Minimal friction (short forms, clear next steps)
- [ ] Mobile optimization
- [ ] Fast loading speed

### Week 7: Email & Ad Copy Optimization

**Email Optimization:**
- Subject line testing (personalization, urgency, curiosity)
- Body copy refinement (shorter, more benefit-focused)
- CTA optimization (clear, action-oriented)
- Sequence flow (timing, number of emails)

**Ad Copy Optimization:**
- Headline testing (different hooks and value props)
- Body copy refinement (clear, concise, benefit-focused)
- CTA optimization (strong, action-oriented)
- Landing page alignment (ad copy matches page)

### Week 8: Messaging Consistency & Training

**Messaging Audit:**
- Review all customer-facing copy for consistency
- Ensure alignment with brand voice and value proposition
- Identify and fix inconsistencies

**Team Training:**
- Brand voice and messaging guidelines
- Copywriting best practices
- Conversion optimization principles
- A/B testing methodology

### Goals
1. Measure impact of optimization efforts
2. Scale successful tests and optimizations
3. Improve overall conversion rates
4. Report on ROI and business impact

### Week 9: Performance Analysis

**Conversion Metrics to Track:**
- **Website:**

  - Bounce rate
  - Time on page
  - Form completion rate

- **Emails:**
  - Open rate

**A/B Test Results Analysis:**

- Winning variations and why they worked
- Losing variations and lessons learned
- Recommendations for next tests

### Week 10: Scaling Successful Optimizations

**Scale What Works:**
- Implement winning A/B test variations across site
- Apply successful messaging to new pages and campaigns
- Expand high-performing ad copy to new audiences
- Roll out optimized email sequences to all segments

**New Tests to Run:**
- Test on lower-priority pages
- Test new messaging angles
- Test different audience segments
- Test new formats (video, interactive, etc.)

### Week 11: Advanced Conversion Projects

**Project 1: Sales Page Optimization**
- Complete redesign of sales page
- New messaging and value proposition
- Improved social proof
- Stronger CTAs

**Project 2: Checkout Flow Optimization**
- Reduce friction in checkout process

- Add trust signals
- Test different payment options

**Project 3: Lead Nurture Sequence**
- Develop new email sequence for leads
- Align with buyer's journey
- Strong CTAs and next steps
- A/B test different approaches

### Week 12: ROI Reporting & Planning

**Impact Measurement:**
- Conversion rate improvements (before vs. after)
- Additional leads generated from optimizations

- Additional revenue attributed

**ROI Calculation:**
- Cost of optimization efforts (time, tools, etc.)

- ROI = (Additional Revenue - Cost) / Cost

**Reporting:**
- Comprehensive 90-day report
- Conversion rate improvements by page
- A/B test results and insights
- Recommendations for next quarter

**Next Quarter Planning:**
- New A/B tests to run
- Pages to optimize
- Messaging to refine
- Tools or resources needed

## Copywriting Frameworks

### 1. AIDA Framework
- **Attention:** Grab attention with a compelling headline
- **Interest:** Generate interest with benefits and value
- **Desire:** Create desire by addressing pain points
- **Action:** Drive action with a clear CTA

### 2. PAS Framework
- **Problem:** Identify the customer's problem
- **Agitate:** Agitate the problem (make it urgent)
- **Solve:** Present your solution

### 3. BAB Framework
- **Before:** Describe the world before your product
- **After:** Describe the world after your product
- **Bridge:** Explain how your product gets them there

### 4. 4 Ps Framework
- **Promise:** What you're promising to deliver
- **Picture:** Paint a picture of the outcome
- **Proof:** Provide social proof or evidence
- **Push:** Push them to take action

### 5. 5Ws Framework
- **Who:** Who is this for?
- **What:** What are you offering?
- **Why:** Why should they care?
- **When:** When should they act?
- **Where:** Where should they go next?
