## Pricing Philosophy

### Value-Based Pricing (Primary Model)
**Core Principle**: Price based on **client ROI**, not time or effort.

**Formula:**
```
Your Price = (Client's Expected Pipeline from Content) × (Your Value Share)
```

**Example Calculation:**
- Client expects $50K in influenced pipeline from content
- You take 10-15% credit for that pipeline
- **Your Price = $5,000-$7,500/month**

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Aligns your incentives with client success
- ✅ Justifies premium pricing ($7K+/month)
- ✅ Attracts serious clients with real budgets
- ✅ Focuses conversations on results, not hours

## Pricing Models Comparison

### 1. Retainer Model (Recommended for $7K+/month)

**Best For:**
- Ongoing content needs
- Long-term partnerships
- Predictable revenue
- Strategic relationships

**Pros:**
- ✅ Predictable, recurring revenue
- ✅ Builds long-term client relationships
- ✅ Encourages strategic, long-term thinking
- ✅ Lower client acquisition cost over time
- ✅ Easier to forecast and plan

**Cons:**
- ❌ Requires consistent delivery
- ❌ Scope creep can be an issue
- ❌ Need to maintain quality over time

**Retainer Structure:**
```
Month 1: 30-50% upfront deposit
Monthly: Fixed retainer fee (auto-billed)
Annual: 10-15% discount for upfront payment
```

### 2. Project-Based Model

**Best For:**
- One-time projects
- Testing new clients
- Specific, defined scope
- Budget-conscious clients

**Pros:**
- ✅ Clear scope and deliverables
- ✅ Easy to sell and understand
- ✅ Good for one-time needs
- ✅ Lower risk for client

**Cons:**
- ❌ Less predictable revenue
- ❌ No recurring income
- ❌ Need to constantly find new projects
- ❌ Harder to build long-term relationships

**Project Structure:**
```
30-50% deposit upon contract signing
30-50% upon delivery of first milestone
20-30% upon final delivery
```

### 3. Fractional Leadership Model

**Best For:**
- Companies needing part-time leadership
- High-growth companies without full-time needs
- Executive-level strategic guidance
- Premium pricing ($8K-$20K/month)

**Pros:**
- ✅ High-value, high-margin work
- ✅ Strategic impact on business
- ✅ Long-term relationships
- ✅ Premium positioning

**Cons:**
- ❌ Requires deep expertise
- ❌ Limited by time availability
- ❌ High expectations from clients

**Fractional Structure:**
```
Monthly retainer: $8,000-$20,000
Hours: 10-20 hours/week
Term: 3-12 month commitment
```

### 4. Hybrid Model (Recommended for Scaling)

**Structure:**
- **Base Retainer**: $5,000-$8,000/month (covers core services)
- **Project Add-Ons**: $1,000-$5,000 (one-time projects)
- **Hourly Consulting**: $200-$400/hour (for ad-hoc needs)

**Example Client:**
```
Base Retainer: $7,000/month (8 blog posts, strategy, SEO)
+ Website Copy Project: $3,000 (one-time)
+ Rush Fee: $500 (for urgent request)
= $10,500 total for the month
```

## Payment Terms

### Standard Payment Terms

**For New Clients:**
- **Deposit**: 30-50% upfront (non-refundable for custom work)
- **Milestone Payments**: 30-50% upon delivery of key milestones
- **Final Payment**: 20-30% upon completion and client approval

**For Established Clients:**
- **Net 15**: Payment due within 15 days of invoice
- **Net 30**: Payment due within 30 days of invoice (for long-term clients only)
- **Auto-Pay**: Automatic monthly payments for retainers

**Standard Terms:**
- **Grace Period**: 3 days (no fee)
- **Late Fee**: 1.5% per month (18% APR) on overdue balances
- **Reminder**: Email reminder at 7 days overdue
- **Final Notice**: Email + phone call at 14 days overdue
- **Work Stoppage**: All work stops at 15 days overdue
- **Collections**: Sent to collections agency at 30 days overdue

### Refund Policy

**Standard Refund Policy:**
- **Deposits**: Non-refundable for custom work (covers time and resources allocated)
- **Milestone Payments**: Non-refundable once work has begun on that milestone
- **Final Payments**: Non-refundable upon delivery
- **Retainers**: No refunds for unused time (but can be credited to future months with 30 days notice)

**Exceptions:**
- Client cancels within 24 hours of contract signing: Full refund (minus payment processing fees)
- Client provides written notice of dissatisfaction within 7 days of delivery: 50% refund on final payment
- Force majeure events: Case-by-case basis

## How to Present Pricing to Clients

### The Value-First Approach

**Step 1: Understand Their Goals**
- "What are your top 3 business goals for the next 12 months?"
- "What does success look like for your content marketing?"
- "What's your current pipeline and revenue from content?"

**Step 2: Quantify the Opportunity**
- "Based on your goals, if we could generate $50K in additional pipeline through content, what would that be worth to you?"
- "What's your current lead-to-customer conversion rate?"
- "What's your average deal size?"

**Step 3: Position Your Solution**
- "My clients typically see 8-12x ROI on their content investment. For a $7,000/month retainer, that's $56K-$84K in pipeline value."
- "I help companies like yours generate predictable pipeline through data-driven content strategies."
- "The investment is a fraction of the return you'll see."

**Step 4: Present Options**
- Offer 2-3 package options (Good, Better, Best)
- Highlight the value and ROI of each
- Recommend the best fit for their situation

**Step 5: Handle Objections**
- **"That's expensive."** → "Compared to what? The ROI is 8-12x, so it's actually a very smart investment."

## Pricing FAQ

### Q: Why are your rates so high?
**A**: "My rates reflect the value I deliver. My clients typically see 8-12x ROI on their investment, which means for every $1 they spend, they get $8-$12 back in pipeline and revenue. When you think about it that way, my rates are actually a very smart investment."

### Q: Can you do it for less?
**A**: "I understand budget is a consideration. I have a few options for you:
1. We can start with a smaller project to test the waters
2. We can reduce the scope to fit your budget
3. I can refer you to a more junior professional who might fit your budget

However, I want to be upfront that reducing the investment will likely reduce the results you see. My full approach is designed to deliver maximum ROI."

### Q: What's your hourly rate?
**A**: "I don't typically work on an hourly basis, as I focus on delivering results rather than tracking time. My pricing is based on the value I provide and the ROI you'll see. That said, if you need an hourly rate for budgeting purposes, my effective rate works out to about $200-$400/hour, depending on the project."

### Q: Do you offer discounts?
**A**: "I do offer discounts in certain situations:
- Annual retainers (10-15% discount for upfront payment)
- Multi-project engagements (10% discount)
- Referrals from existing clients (5-10% discount)
- Non-profit organizations (20-30% discount)

However, I don't discount my rates just to win business. My pricing is based on the value I deliver, and I want to work with clients who see that value."

### Q: What if we're not happy with the results?
**A**: "I stand behind my work and am confident in the results I can deliver. That said, business outcomes depend on many factors, and I can't guarantee specific results. What I can guarantee is that I'll work hard to deliver the best possible strategy and content, and I'll be transparent about progress and performance.

If you're not happy with the work, we can discuss adjustments to the approach. And if we're not a good fit, we can part ways with no hard feelings (though I'll do everything I can to make it work)."

### Q: Can we start with a smaller project?
**A**: "Absolutely! Many of my clients start with a smaller project to test the waters. A content audit or a one-time strategy project is a great way to see the value I can provide before committing to a larger engagement.

That said, I want to be upfront that a smaller project will have a smaller impact. My full retainer packages are designed to deliver maximum ROI, and that's where you'll see the best results."

### Q: How do you measure ROI?
**A**: "I measure ROI in several ways, depending on your goals:

1. **Pipeline Generated**: Track the number of leads and opportunities influenced by content
2. **Revenue Attributed**: Measure the revenue generated from content-driven leads
3. **Organic Traffic Growth**: Track increases in website traffic from SEO
4. **Lead Quality**: Measure the conversion rate from lead to customer
5. **Engagement Metrics**: Track time on page, scroll depth, social shares, etc.

I provide regular reports showing these metrics and the ROI of your investment. Most of my clients see 8-12x ROI within 90 days."

## Pricing Adjustment Strategy

### When to Raise Your Rates

**Signs It's Time to Raise Rates:**
- ✅ You're consistently booked 2-3 months in advance
- ✅ You're turning away more clients than you're accepting
- ✅ Your clients are seeing exceptional results (15x+ ROI)
- ✅ You have strong case studies and testimonials
- ✅ Demand for your services has increased
- ✅ You've added new skills or certifications
- ✅ It's been 6-12 months since your last rate increase

**How to Raise Rates:**
1. **For New Clients**: Simply update your pricing on your website and proposals
2. **For Existing Clients**: Grandfather them in at current rates, but offer to upgrade to new packages
3. **Announcement**: "I'm excited to announce that due to increased demand and the exceptional results I've been delivering for clients, I'll be raising my rates for new clients starting [Date]."

**Rate Increase Schedule:**
- **Year 1**: Start at market rate ($5K-$7K/month)
- **Year 2**: Increase to $7K-$10K/month
- **Year 3**: Increase to $10K-$15K/month
- **Year 4+**: $15K-$20K+/month (or transition to agency model)

### When to Lower Your Rates (Rarely)

**Acceptable Reasons to Lower Rates:**
- Entering a new market or niche (temporary discount)
- Building case studies in a new industry
- Strategic partnership with long-term potential
- Non-profit or educational organizations

**Unacceptable Reasons to Lower Rates:**
- ❌ Client can't afford your rates
- ❌ Client is difficult or demanding
- ❌ Project doesn't align with your expertise
- ❌ Client doesn't value your work
