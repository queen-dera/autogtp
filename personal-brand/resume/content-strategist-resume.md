## Resume Formatting Guide

### ATS-Friendly Formatting
- **File Type**: PDF (preferred) or Word
- **Font**: Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman (11-12pt)
- **Length**: 1 page (under 10 years experience), 2 pages (10+ years)
- **Margins**: 0.5-1 inch
- **File Name**: `FirstName-LastName-Content-Strategist-Resume.pdf`

### Structure
```
[Header]
[Professional Summary]
[Key Achievements/Snapshot]
[Professional Experience]
[Skills]
[Education]
[Certifications]
[Additional Information]
```

## Resume Tips for $7K+/Month Roles

### 1. Quantify Everything
- Instead of: "Created content strategy"
- Use: "Built content strategy that generated $50K+ in pipeline within 90 days"

### 2. Focus on Business Impact
- Instead of: "Wrote blog posts"
- Use: "Wrote 20+ blog posts that generated 50,000+ page views and 200+ MQLs"

### 3. Show Leadership
- Instead of: "Worked on content team"
- Use: "Led content team of 5 to produce 16 pieces/month with 15% conversion rate"

### 4. Highlight Strategy
- Instead of: "Managed social media"
- Use: "Developed social media strategy that grew followers by 200% and engagement by 300%"

### 5. Include Relevant Metrics
- Traffic growth
- Lead generation
- Conversion rates
- Pipeline influenced
- Revenue generated
- ROI
- Cost savings
- Time savings

### 6. Tailor for Each Application
- Customize for **Content Strategist** vs. **Content Marketer** vs. **Copywriter**
- Adjust based on **company size** (startup vs. enterprise)
- Align with **industry** (B2B SaaS vs. agency vs. e-commerce)

### 7. Use Action Verbs
- Built
- Developed
- Created
- Implemented
- Led
- Managed
- Optimized
- Increased
- Generated
- Improved

### 8. Keep It Concise
- 1 page for <10 years experience
- 2 pages for 10+ years
- Bullet points, not paragraphs
- Remove irrelevant experience

### 9. Include Relevant Links
- LinkedIn profile
- Portfolio website
- Published articles
- Case studies
- Social media profiles

### 10. Proofread
- No typos or grammatical errors
- Consistent formatting
- Professional tone
- ATS-friendly (avoid tables, images, fancy formatting)
