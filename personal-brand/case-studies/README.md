## Case Study Best Practices

### 1. Structure
Every case study should include:
- **Title**: Clear, benefit-focused headline
- **Client Overview**: Brief description of the client
- **Challenge**: The problem or opportunity
- **Solution**: What you did to address it
- **Results**: Quantifiable outcomes
- **Testimonial**: Client quote (if available)
- **Key Takeaways**: Lessons learned

### 2. Content
**Do:**
- Use specific numbers and metrics
- Focus on business impact (pipeline, revenue, etc.)
- Tell a compelling story
- Include visuals (charts, graphs, screenshots)
- Keep it concise (1-2 pages max)

**Don't:**
- Be vague about results
- Focus only on vanity metrics
- Make it too long or complex
- Forget to include a clear CTA
- Use generic, non-specific language

### 3. Design
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Use headings, bullet points, and bold text
- **Branding**: Include your logo and branding
- **Visuals**: Add charts, graphs, and images
- **Readability**: Use short paragraphs and plenty of white space
- **CTA**: Include a clear next step (contact you, download more, etc.)

### 4. Metrics to Include
**Traffic Metrics:**
- Organic traffic growth
- Direct traffic growth
- Social traffic growth
- Total page views

**Engagement Metrics:**
- Time on page
- Bounce rate
- Social shares
- Comments and interactions

**Conversion Metrics:**
- Form submissions
- Email signups
- Demo requests
- Downloads

**Business Metrics:**
- Leads generated
- Pipeline influenced
- Revenue attributed
- ROI

## Customizing Case Studies

### For Real Clients
If you have real client work to showcase:
1. **Get Permission**: Always ask the client for permission to use their name and results
2. **Protect Confidentiality**: Remove or anonymize sensitive information
3. **Focus on Results**: Highlight the business impact you delivered
4. **Tell Their Story**: Frame the case study around the client's success

### For Hypothetical Case Studies
If you don't have real client work yet:
1. **Use Your Portfolio**: Base case studies on your $7K SaaS Content Strategy Portfolio
2. **Be Transparent**: Clearly label as "Hypothetical" or "Example"
3. **Use Real Data**: Base results on industry benchmarks and your strategy
4. **Show Your Process**: Demonstrate your methodology and approach

### For Different Roles
**Content Strategist:**
- Focus on strategy development, ROI, and business impact
- Highlight framework creation and implementation

**Content Marketing Specialist:**
- Focus on execution, optimization, and lead generation
- Highlight content creation and distribution

**Conversion Copywriter:**
- Focus on messaging, A/B testing, and conversion optimization
- Highlight copy improvements and their impact

**Fractional Leader:**
- Focus on leadership, team building, and strategic direction
- Highlight cross-functional collaboration and business impact
